## The mechanic behind Walmart cashback

Walmart is not handing you money out of generosity. It pays affiliate commissions to marketing partners for referred sales, the same way it pays a search engine or a coupon site. When you start a shopping session on ShopBack and click through to Walmart, a tracking cookie tags your session. If you check out, Walmart's affiliate network confirms the sale and pays ShopBack a commission. ShopBack keeps part of it and passes the rest back to you as cashback.

Three things follow from that:

- Your Walmart price is identical whether you go through ShopBack or type walmart.com directly. The commission comes out of a marketing budget that already existed.
- ShopBack does not charge you. No subscription, no service fee, no withdrawal fee on standard payouts. That is not charitable — the merchant funds it.
- Cashback arrives late, by design. Walmart holds it as Pending until the return window closes. For Walmart, that window is long.

## The Walmart-specific rule that breaks most orders

Every merchant has quirks. Walmart's is the cart.

ShopBack's Walmart terms spell out that you need to clear your cart and close the Walmart app before starting your session, then visit Walmart from ShopBack and complete the purchase there. And you need to do it again for the next order. If you had twelve items sitting in your Walmart cart from last week and you click through ShopBack and check out, you are gambling on whether the track records.

There are four other failure points worth knowing about, all listed in the merchant terms:

- **Ad blockers, VPNs, and third-party links.** Facebook ads, Google Ads, another cashback extension, a coupon site link — any of these can steal or break the attribution. If a chain of links is involved, ShopBack is usually the loser.
- **Cookies.** Walmart only confirms a transaction if you accepted all cookies on its pages. Deny them and the sale may never confirm.
- **Interrupted sessions.** If Walmart throws an app download prompt or a payment error mid-checkout, restart the visit from ShopBack rather than retrying on the Walmart side.
- **Non-featured promo codes.** A code you found somewhere else can void the commission entirely.

None of this is hard, but it is a sequence, not a habit you can skip. The mental model that works: for Walmart, the path is ShopBack → empty cart → checkout, every single time.

## What Walmart purchases never earn cashback

Walmart's Walmart-branded exclusions are straightforward once you see them collected in one place:

- Gift cards, vouchers, and store credit purchases
- Gift card or voucher redemption
- Store credit redemption
- Purchases paid with vouchers or promo codes not featured on ShopBack
- Taxes, service charges, shipping, and delivery fees
- Anything cancelled, refunded, exchanged, or returned

Partial returns get prorated, so a $200 order where you send back $50 worth of items adjusts the cashback downward rather than cancelling it.

The taxes and shipping exclusion is where the real-world value shrinks. On a $60 grocery delivery order with $8 in fees and tax, your earning base is roughly $52. At 1%, that is about 50 cents. Walmart cashback is a volume game, not a single-purchase game — which is exactly why the boosts matter so much more than the base rate.

## Getting the money out

Withdrawal rules on the US platform are specific, and they trip people up in predictable ways.

- **Minimum:** $10 in confirmed cashback. Bonus cashback does not count toward the minimum the same way order cashback does — the support documentation is explicit that you can withdraw bonus cashback once your confirmed cashback reaches $10.
- **Daily cap:** $300 per day.
- **Methods:** PayPal or ACH transfer to a linked bank account. The name on the payout account has to match your ShopBack account.
- **Timing:** typically within 10 calendar days, and in some cases up to 30 days, because payouts are processed in batches and depend on the payment partner. Federal holidays add delays.
- **Expiry:** none. Confirmed cashback does not expire in your balance, so withdrawing every time an order confirms and letting it build up are equally valid strategies.

One practical warning from user reports: keep your payout details current. Incorrect PayPal or bank details are the leading cause of delayed withdrawals, and a bounced payout means re-verifying from scratch.

## Questions people actually ask

**Does Walmart in-store shopping earn cashback?** Treat ShopBack US as an online-earning platform. The Walmart terms are written around online carts, cookies, and checkout sessions, and the Walmart flow assumes you are buying on the site or in the app.

**Why is my Walmart cashback still pending after two months?** Because Walmart confirms in roughly 75 days. Nothing you can do speeds it up. If it is still pending past that and appears genuine, support can investigate with your order number and purchase date.

**Can I use a promo code and still earn cashback?** Only if the code is featured on ShopBack. An outside code can void the commission, and you will not know until the order fails to confirm.

**Is the $10 withdrawal minimum per withdrawal or per balance?** It is the confirmed balance threshold. Bonus cashback alone will not get you past it.

**Does cashback expire if I forget to withdraw?** No. Confirmed cashback stays in your balance until you request a payout.
